# Getting started with Findable

Welcome to the Findable pilot. Findable helps your business show up well when
visitors ask AI assistants (like ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot) where to stay,
eat or what to do.

## What happens in the pilot

- **We check how AI assistants describe your business** today, and what's missing or wrong.
- **We agree a short action plan with you.** It appears in your portal under **Action plan**.
- **Every week you get a brief:** what changed, why it matters, and the next steps.
- **These are observations, not guarantees.** AI assistants change their answers often. What we report is what we saw when we checked.

## Your first sign-in

1. Open the invitation email from Findable and choose a password (12+ characters). The link works once and lasts 7 days.
2. Sign in at **[APP ADDRESS]** with your email and new password.
3. Your **Overview** shows your next actions, latest brief and results.

## Using the portal

- **Overview:** what to do next, and your latest brief.
- **Action plan:** everything we've agreed, in priority order. We mark items done as you complete them.
- **Reports:** all your weekly briefs.
- **Account & team:** your business details, who has access, and your sign-in security.
- **Send update:** tell us about anything new (bookings, reviews, changes). It goes straight to the Findable team.

## Your information

Only people from your business and the Findable team can see your
information. Other businesses can't. See our privacy policy at
**[APP ADDRESS]/privacy**.
